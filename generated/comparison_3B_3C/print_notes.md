# Combined #3B + #3C A1 plate

One of each, left to right: **#3B curved press-fit**, then **#3C side-by-side press-fit**. #3B prints on its flat long side; #3C prints flat. Bodies have a 12 mm gap.

A1 / Generic PLA / 0.4 mm / 0.20 mm Standard / Textured PEI / by layer / supports disabled.

Native slice: 25.1 minutes, 4.54 g PLA, 85 layers. No slice warnings, support toolpaths, or floating layer islands.

Open connectors_3B_3C_A1_PLA.3mf in Bambu Studio and click Slice plate. The editable project contains both meshes; the separately saved validated_toolpaths.gcode is the native slice used for verification.
