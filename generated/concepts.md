# Wearable USB-C Retainer Concepts

All concepts keep both USB-C ends facing each other on the same plane and assume a typical connector envelope:

- Plug envelope: 6.8 x 8.5 x 2.8 mm
- Housing envelope: 8.8 x 12.0 x 6.4 mm
- Cable pass-through allowance: 4.5 x 3.2 mm

## Straight bar / Push-in socket

- Form note: Compact linear spine with mirrored inward-facing pockets.
- Overall dimensions: 46.00 x 18.00 x 8.20 mm
- Insertion / removal: Straight axial push-in from each outer end. Removal is a direct pull while the outer throat relaxes enough to let the housing pass back out.
- Firm plug retention: Firm plug retention happens at the close-fit inner plug pocket and the shoulder between the housing chamber and the smaller plug recess.
- Moderate housing retention: Moderate housing retention comes from the recessed mouth being smaller than the housing envelope, while still large enough for repeated snap-in use.
- Better suited for: Best for firmer TPU or semi-rigid elastomer. The body stays more solid and relies on a shallow snap throat instead of large flex windows.
- STEP: `straight_bar_push_socket.step`
- STL: `straight_bar_push_socket.stl`

## Straight bar / Press-fit recess

- Form note: Compact linear spine with mirrored inward-facing pockets.
- Overall dimensions: 46.00 x 18.00 x 8.20 mm
- Insertion / removal: Axial press-in through a narrower snap throat. Removal uses a peel-and-pull motion so the relief windows can open before the housing clears.
- Firm plug retention: Firm plug retention comes from the same stepped plug pocket, but with a tighter entry throat so the tip stays deep in the blind recess during wear.
- Moderate housing retention: Moderate housing retention is handled by the compliant snap throat and flanking relief slots so the housing is captured without needing a hard rigid clamp.
- Better suited for: Best for soft TPU or silicone-like elastomer. The retention comes from a more aggressive snap mouth backed by side relief slots.
- STEP: `straight_bar_press_fit.step`
- STL: `straight_bar_press_fit.stl`

## Straight bar / Side-entry slide-in

- Form note: Compact linear spine with mirrored inward-facing pockets.
- Overall dimensions: 46.00 x 18.00 x 8.20 mm
- Insertion / removal: Lateral slide-in through mirrored side channels, then a short inward push seats the plug against the blind stop. Removal reverses that slide while the lips flex.
- Firm plug retention: Firm plug retention occurs at the blind inner plug pocket; the plug cannot translate far enough to escape unless the whole connector is first slid back to the side slot.
- Moderate housing retention: Moderate housing retention comes from top and bottom lips over the side channel, which keep the housing captured while leaving insertion force manageable.
- Better suited for: Best for medium-flex elastomer. It splits the difference between secure snap-in retention and easier removal.
- STEP: `straight_bar_side_entry.step`
- STL: `straight_bar_side_entry.stl`
