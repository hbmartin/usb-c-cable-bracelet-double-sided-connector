# Compact USB-C retainers — revision 2

Three separate one-piece holders. Do not assemble them together.

Plate, left to right: **#3 press-fit · #5 side-entry · #9 ribbed push-in**.
Centers: (92,128), (128,128), (164,128) mm; 12 mm gaps.

Bodies: 24 × 17 × 8.4 mm. Housing capture: 3.2 mm per end. Pocket depth: 10.8 mm per end. Center divider: 2.4 mm.
Metal envelope: 6.8 × 8.5 × 2.8 mm. Plastic housing: 12 mm wide × 6.4 mm thick.
A1 / 0.4 mm / Generic PLA / 0.20 mm Standard / Textured PEI. Print flat.
Actual insertion force, grip, and fatigue require testing with your cable.

## #3 Compact press-fit

- Use: Push plugs into opposite ends, metal tips toward the center. Only the front 3.2 mm of each plastic housing is captured. Test PLA flex without forcing it.
- Retention: Original #3 cavity/mouth widths and heights, a shorter 0.8 mm throat, and 0.8 mm flanking relief slots.
- STEP: `step/03_compact_press_fit.step`
- STL: `stl/03_compact_press_fit.stl`

## #5 Compact side-entry

- Use: Slide the housing and metal tip sideways through the stepped opening, then move the plug 0.8 mm inward. Reverse that move before sliding out.
- Retention: Mirrored side entrances with lead-ins, localized rounded housing detents (6.25 mm gap), and a closed side wall capturing the last 0.8 mm of the tip.
- STEP: `step/05_compact_side_entry.step`
- STL: `stl/05_compact_side_entry.stl`

## #9 Compact ribbed push-in

- Use: Push each plug into an end. Rounded ribs grip the plastic housing; most of the housing remains exposed.
- Retention: Two opposing rib pairs per end project 0.2 mm, span 8 mm, have 0.8 mm axial bases, and sit 1.3/2.5 mm inside the opening. Metal-tip pockets stay smooth.
- STEP: `step/09_compact_ribbed_push_in.step`
- STL: `stl/09_compact_ribbed_push_in.stl`
