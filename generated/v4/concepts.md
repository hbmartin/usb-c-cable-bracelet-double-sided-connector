# #3C Side-by-side press-fit

Flat body: 13 × 30 × 8.4 mm. Two parallel sockets, 13 mm apart, entered from opposite ends.

Push one plug into each opposite end, along the two parallel socket axes. The plugs' wide faces stay parallel to the top; cables exit opposite ends.

Each lane preserves #3's straight socket geometry: metal 8.65 × 2.95 mm, housing 12.20 × 6.60 mm, mouth 11.25 × 5.65 mm, nominal throat 0.8 mm with the existing 0.15 mm transition overlap, and 0.8 mm relief slots. Pocket depth: 10.8 mm. Housing capture: 3.2 mm.

Closed end walls: 2.2 mm. Minimum inter-lane void separation: 1.044 mm. Minimum enclosed relief-slot wall: 0.800 mm.

Print flat at (128, 128) mm: A1 / Generic PLA / 0.4 mm / 0.20 mm Standard / Textured PEI; supports disabled.

Physical acceptance: both plugs retain #3's usable fit independently, stay seated when the other is inserted or removed, and cause no cracking around the shared structure. These require a print test.
