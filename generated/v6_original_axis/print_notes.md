# #3E A1 print notes

One centered **standard-fit #3E holder**. Try the separate fit samples before printing this holder.

A1 / Generic PLA / 0.4 mm / 0.20 mm Standard / Textured PEI. 20% gyroid, three walls, five top/bottom layers, 1 mm shells. Print on the flat negative-X end face, −82.5° about Y; supports disabled.

Native slice: 19.1 minutes, 2.50 g PLA, 72 layers. No warnings, support toolpaths, floating islands, or blocked socket-core probes.

Open the editable 3MF in Bambu Studio and click Slice plate. The project has no cached G-code. Geometry and slicing do not measure insertion force, retention, or fatigue. Stop if insertion requires excessive force or causes damage.

Projects are prepared only; this workflow does not connect to or start the printer.
